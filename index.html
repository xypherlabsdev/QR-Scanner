1.Who we are

QRScan Pro is developed and published by Xypher Labs, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the QRScan Pro Android app ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies together with our Terms & Conditions.

2.Data that stays on your device

The following is processed and stored only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We do not receive it.

Camera

The camera is used only while the scanner is open, to find and read QR codes and barcodes. Detection runs on the device with Google ML Kit. No video is recorded and no camera frames are uploaded.

Photo of each scan

When a code is scanned, the app keeps a photo of what the camera saw (or a reduced-size copy of the gallery image you picked) so it can show it next to the scan in History. These photos are stored only in the app's private folder, are not visible to other apps, and are deleted when you delete that History entry.

Images from your gallery

If you choose to scan an image, the app reads only the one image you pick through Android's file picker. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library.

History

The content of codes you scan or create, their type, date and time, favourites, and the style of QR codes you create (colours, shapes) are stored in a database on your device so you can search and reopen them.

Settings and app state

Your language, scan sound, vibration and auto-open settings, whether you have finished the intro and your ad consent status are stored on the device. Images for promotions of our own apps are cached there too.

Link safety hints

When a scanned link looks risky (for example, it uses a number address instead of a site name), the app warns you. This check runs entirely on the device; links are not sent anywhere to be checked.

Wi-Fi codes

When you tap Connect on a Wi-Fi code, the network name and password from that code are passed to Android's own "Add network" screen. We do not receive them.

Saved QR images

When you tap Save, the QR image is written to your phone's gallery (Pictures/QRScanPro).

When you choose to leave the app

Some buttons hand information to another app or website that you choose, under that app's or site's own policy:

  • Search product / Search web: opens Google Search in your browser with the barcode number or text of the code.
  • Open: opens a scanned link, phone number, email, map or payment code in the app you pick.
  • Share: sends the code to the app you pick. The shared text, or the caption of a shared QR image, includes a link to QRScan Pro on Google Play.
  • Rate us: opens the app's Google Play page.

4.How the data is used

  • To provide scanning, code creation, History, sharing and the other features of the app.
  • To show ads, personalised only where you have consented or where the law allows it, and to limit how often they appear.
  • To measure usage, find crashes and improve the app.
  • To respect your consent choices and meet legal obligations.

Where the GDPR applies, the legal bases are your consent (personalised ads, analytics where required), our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure (crash reports, basic configuration), and legal obligations.

5.Advertising and your consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. If you decline, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • California and other US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising through the consent form or Settings → Privacy Options, when that option is shown.
  • Other regions: personalised ads may be shown, depending on your device's ad settings.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy Options (shown where it applies). On your phone you can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, wording varies by device), and manage Google ad personalisation at My Ad Center.

The app currently has no in-app purchases.

7.Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above so they can run the app's ads, analytics and crash reporting, when the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of users or others, or with your consent.

8.Retention and deletion

  • On your device: History, scan photos and settings stay until you delete them. You can delete entries in History (swipe one, or long-press to select several), clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: kept for the retention period set in our Firebase project, up to 14 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are kept for 90 days.
  • AdMob and UMP: kept according to Google's policies.

Because the app has no accounts, we cannot link data held by Google to you by name. If you want help with a request, email us (see Contact).

9.Security

On-device data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Data sent to the services above travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

10.Your rights

  • India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023): you can ask for information about how your personal data is processed, ask for it to be corrected or erased, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance with us.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR, Swiss FADP): rights of access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, the right to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Privacy Options), and the right to complain to your data protection authority.
  • California and other US states with privacy laws: rights to know, delete and correct personal information, to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.

Email us to use any of these rights. We reply within 30 days.

11.International transfers

We do not knowingly collect data from children below that age. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will help remove what we can.
